# By JTT Article Style Guide

Every public article uses one shared visual and semantic grammar, with sections varying by article type.

## Required metadata

Show published date/time, last updated, last verified, next review where present, author/byline, reading time, canonical URL and topic/vertical context.

## Article anatomy

Use one H1. Lead with a concise answer/dek, then the type-specific structure from `config/article-templates.json`. Use short paragraphs, descriptive H2/H3 headings, tables where comparison is clearer than prose, and explicit caveats for uncertainty.

## Evidence

Factual claims should be traceable to source and evidence identifiers in the publication model. Community discussion can provide useful context, sentiment and discovery leads, but must not be presented as independently verified fact without corroboration.

## Images

Use a purposeful hero where appropriate and evidence/diagram/benchmark images when they improve comprehension. Every image needs meaningful alt text and a provenance/licence state. Avoid decorative stock imagery that adds no information.

## Links

Prefer stable canonical internal links to related By JTT research. External links should point directly to the useful primary source when possible. Avoid link stuffing.

## Search and agent readability

Keep the central answer explicit. Preserve dates, versions, source names and limitations in visible text. Emit machine-readable metadata from the same publication record; never maintain a competing metadata-only truth.

## Corrections and updates

When evidence changes, update the affected claim and the visible update/verification timestamps. Material contradictions should be preserved in research history rather than silently rewriting the historical conclusion.
