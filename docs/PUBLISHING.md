# Research Publishing Contract

The lab should generate publishable first-party research as a by-product of real development work. The public destination is games.byjtt.com.

## Public research must include

- the question/hypothesis;
- test date;
- tested versions and important configuration;
- methodology and fairness constraints;
- hardware/environment where relevant;
- results with raw or linked evidence where appropriate;
- failures and limitations;
- measured facts separated from interpretation;
- clear recommendation scoped to the tested context;
- source/provenance/licence notes;
- update/supersession status when newer evidence exists.

## Preferred content families

- `/research/` — deeper investigations and methodology;
- `/benchmarks/` — controlled comparisons;
- `/experiments/` — smaller focused tests and prototypes;
- `/games/` — shipped or graduating games;
- `/lab/` — methodology, status, and technology leaderboard;
- `/tools/` — reusable public tools/components where appropriate.

## SEO/GEO principles

Optimise for usefulness and primary evidence rather than keyword volume. Research titles should clearly describe the tested question and year/version where freshness matters. Pages should expose structured facts, comparison data, methodology, conclusions, and links to playable evidence/source where safe.

Do not fabricate benchmark numbers, reviews, player results, quotations, or performance claims. Do not publish private telemetry or commercially sensitive information by default.

## Experiment publication package

Each completed public experiment should be capable of producing:

1. canonical research article;
2. concise result summary;
3. comparison tables/charts from structured result data;
4. screenshots and gameplay capture;
5. playable builds where suitable;
6. source/evidence links;
7. social-preview copy/assets;
8. changelog/update path when later experiments supersede the conclusion.

The website is an output of the lab, not the source of truth. Raw experiment evidence remains versioned with the experiment or in an explicitly linked evidence store.
