# By JTT Editorial + SEO Review Protocol

## Goal

Make every public article useful to humans first, trustworthy to agents, and technically discoverable without turning the publication system into a manual editorial job.

## Review sequence

`evidence gate → writer/editor critique → SEO/search critique → accessibility/agent-readability gate → publish`

The same publication record remains canonical. Reviewers critique the generated projection; they do not become a second source of truth.

## Editorial critique

A publication-quality writer/editor should assess:

1. **Answer-first clarity** — the main question is answered near the top in plain language.
2. **Original value** — the article adds research, synthesis, testing, comparison, methodology or practical insight rather than paraphrasing commodity summaries.
3. **Reader usefulness** — a reader can understand what to do, what changed, or what the evidence means.
4. **Structure** — one H1, useful H2/H3 hierarchy, short paragraphs, scannable lists/tables, meaningful captions and clear transitions.
5. **Evidence honesty** — fact, inference, vendor claim, community report and By JTT execution are clearly distinguished.
6. **Specificity** — versions, dates, products, constraints and limitations are named where material.
7. **Freshness** — visible publication/update/verification state is accurate.
8. **Voice** — confident but not hype-driven; uncertainty is explicit.
9. **Originality** — no unnecessary duplication of existing By JTT articles; update an existing canonical article when the search intent is already covered.
10. **Human editing** — awkward, repetitive, generic or obviously machine-written passages are revised or rejected.

## SEO/search critique

The SEO evaluator should assess the rendered page against current search-engine fundamentals and schema.org semantics, not a proprietary scorecard. Check:

- search intent is satisfied directly;
- title and description accurately describe the page;
- canonical URL is stable and unique;
- headings describe real content rather than keyword stuffing;
- internal links are useful and contextual;
- external links favour authoritative primary sources;
- structured data matches visible content;
- publication and modification dates are truthful;
- authorship is explicit;
- images have useful alt text and appropriate dimensions;
- page is indexable unless intentionally excluded;
- no doorway, duplicate, autogenerated-spam or misleading-content pattern is introduced;
- article is useful without structured data being present;
- social metadata is valid and points at the canonical page.

## Agent readability

A machine reader should be able to identify the article's question, concise answer, claims, evidence, sources, dates, limitations and related topics without guessing from marketing copy. Structured data is supplemental; visible content remains authoritative for readers.

## Human escalation

Escalate rather than auto-publish when there is legal/licensing ambiguity, unsupported material claims, contradictory evidence, sensitive safety/security content, a significant factual disagreement, or an editorial score below the configured threshold.

## Independence rule

No evaluator may mark a claim verified merely because another evaluator accepted it. Evidence status comes from the research/evidence layer. Editorial and SEO reviews judge presentation and discoverability.

## Maintenance rule

Store review results with the publication revision. Re-review only when content, supporting evidence, search intent, templates or relevant platform guidance materially changes. Routine articles should not require a human checklist on every refresh.
