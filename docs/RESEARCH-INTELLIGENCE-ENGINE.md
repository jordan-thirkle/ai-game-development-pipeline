# By JTT Research & Intelligence Engine

## Purpose

A shared, evidence-first editorial system for Games, Design and Development. It turns research signals into traceable findings and, when eligible, into public articles without creating separate CMSs or duplicated canonical content.

## Source model

The radar may ingest official documentation, standards, releases, GitHub, research publications and community discussion. Reddit, X and other community sources are discovery/sentiment inputs by default. A community claim is never promoted to verified evidence merely because it is repeated or popular.

## Research lifecycle

`signal → cluster → candidate → research → evidence-backed finding → publication/update/hold`

A `candidate` is a research work item, not a verified finding and **MUST NOT be publication-eligible**. Promotion into a candidate queue is intentionally separate from evidence verification. Findings record exact observation dates, source URLs, claims, status and review deadlines. Contradiction or source changes trigger re-evaluation rather than silent overwrites.

## Universal taxonomy

Every record may classify by domain, discipline, technology, audience, content type and source type. Initial domains are Games, Software, Web, Apps and Design. Initial content types are Research, Guide, Tutorial, Benchmark, Comparison, Case Study, Opinion, Trend, News, Standard and Glossary.

## Publication lifecycle

`validated evidence → article model → metadata/media/link QA → vertical index → public site`

One canonical article may be surfaced by multiple verticals. Vertical adapters create indexes and navigation; they do not clone canonical content.

## Low-maintenance policy

Deterministic validation owns structural correctness, evidence lineage, dates, taxonomy and freshness. AI may draft or update prose and social copy only from validated records. High-risk editorial, legal or unsupported claims are held for human review. Existing articles should be updated when new evidence answers the same intent rather than producing near-duplicates.

## SEO and machine readability

Public output should expose stable canonical URLs, publication and modification timestamps, authorship, source/evidence references, descriptive headings, semantic HTML, appropriate structured data, Open Graph/social metadata, accessible images and stable internal links. Machine-readable research records preserve claims, evidence, sources, confidence and freshness so agents can consume the knowledge without reverse-engineering prose.

## Trend radar

Signals can originate from community discussions, product launches, repositories, releases, standards and search-demand indicators. Trend detection creates research candidates; it does not publish rumours. The system prioritises novelty, relevance, evidenceability and urgency.
